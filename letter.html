Happiest Birthdayyy, Dear Shubhanginiii ♡

You’re so much more special to me than you’ll ever understand. I could probably write pages and pages about what you mean to me and still find a hundred more things to say.

You’ve become such an important part of my life that I honestly can’t imagine my days without you in them. Thank you for always understanding me, for staying, and for making me feel safe and happy in ways you probably don’t even realise. I’m genuinely so grateful to have you in my life.

I know there are a thousand ways to wish someone a birthday, but I wanted to wish you a little differently. So, I hope life always gives you the window seat, the extra fries, all your favourite KFC chicken and sweets from every corner of the world.

Green traffic lights when you’re running late, a playlist that knows your mood, friends who start laughing before you even finish the joke, random flowers, your dream job, a full bank account, and your favourite food waiting for you after the longest day. And whenever the world feels a little unkind, I hope someone is there to make it feel a little better. May you always have enough reasons to say, “That was a good day.” And I hope you get everything you never knew how to ask for.

Some people come into your life and become memories, but then there are people who become a part of you. You’re one of those people for me. No matter where life takes us, I hope we always find our way back to each other.

And more than anything, I hope life lets us stay a part of each other’s stories for a long, long time, through all the changes, all the different versions of ourselves, and all the years still waiting for us. I hope you never forget that you’ll never be just another person to me. You’ll always be someone incredibly special, someone I could never replace, and someone I’ll always be thankful to have in my life. I hope you know how deeply you’re respected, valued, cherished, and loved by me.

Once again, the happiest birthday to you, Shubhanginiii! I hope this year brings you more happiness than you can imagine and gives you countless little moments that make life feel beautiful. You deserve all the good things this world has to offer, and I really hope you get to experience them all.
